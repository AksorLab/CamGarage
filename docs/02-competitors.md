# 02. Competitors

Only what I could see on public pages. Nothing here was tested in the app stores.

- **AnkorPark** (https://www.ankorpark.com/). Calls itself "Cambodia's first digital parking platform". The page mentions real-time availability, QR entry, digital payments and automated entry/exit, and Phnom Penh and Siem Reap. The page I fetched is a marketing page with little detail. **[verified]** that it exists and what it claims. **Unknown:** whether it is live, how many lots, whether it has a consumer app.
- **L2R Cambodia / SuperPark (with Keytop)** (https://l2r-khmer.com/solutions-parking). A B2B **ANPR** (number plate recognition) system for lots, with cashless payment, a patron mobile app (payment, bay reservation, car finding, monthly renewal, coupons), BI analytics and a multi-carpark platform covering on-street and enclosed lots. **[verified]**. It sells to operators, so it is a potential partner for data rather than a rival.
- **Keytop Cambodia** (https://keytop-cambodia.com/). Same group. Camera-based parking guidance and CCTV. **[verified]**
- **Mall parking** (e.g. AEON Mall Phnom Penh has a published fee table, but the page returned 403 to me). Malls are the main organised parking supply. **[assumption]**
- **Global map apps** (Google Maps, Parkopedia). **[assumption]** They cover malls well but have poor Cambodian lot data (OSM export suggests the same, see `03`).

## Read-out

- **[assumption]** The gap is probably a **neutral consumer directory** of lots and garages, with hours, price and services, across operators. Operator systems (SuperPark, Keytop) each serve their own sites. We could be the layer that lists all of them, rather than build hardware.
- **Risk:** AnkorPark may already be heading there. Check it by installing it, if there is an app.
