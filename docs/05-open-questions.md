## Suggested validation (cheap)

- Talk to **10 to 20 drivers**: how do they find parking today, what do they pay, what goes wrong.
- Visit **20 lots** and record hours, prices and services. This also tests the data model.
- **Install AnkorPark** and any competitor app and compare.
- Read the JICA report parking chapter.
- Check **Google Places terms** before any data from it is stored.

## Risks

- Stale data. A wrong price or closed lot destroys trust faster than missing data.
- Chicken and egg: drivers want listings, owners want drivers.
- Many users are on motorcycles with informal paid parking. These are hard to map and mostly unpriced.
- Legal and ToS issues from scraping.
