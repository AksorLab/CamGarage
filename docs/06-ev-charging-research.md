# 06 - EV charging in Cambodia (round 3)

Tags: [verified] = primary or multiple news sources, [source-claim] = single or vendor source, [assumption] = our guess.

## Market size
- Aug 2024: 3,676 registered EVs and 21 charging stations nationwide. [source-claim] (construction-property.com, Xinhua, Cambodianess agree on 21 stations)
- Oct 2024: 2,513 electric cars, 1,329 e-motorcycles, 478 e-three-wheelers (MPWT via Xinhua). [verified]
- 2025: nearly 8,000 EVs registered in the year, out of 522K new vehicles. [source-claim] (search snippet only; page 404 on fetch)
- Sep 2026: 22,793 EVs (16,926 cars, 4,974 motorcycles, 893 three-wheelers), within 8.58M vehicles; 460 public and partner-operated chargers. [source-claim] (Cambodianess, quoted by EVCSTAR)
- Policy target 2024-2030: 30,000 e-cars, 720,000 e-motorcycles, 20,000 e-tricycles. [source-claim]
- Implication: EV growth is fast but the base is tiny, and mostly two-wheelers. Cars (our parking users) are about 17K.

## Regulation
- Prakas 0389 (June 2025): MME gives permits and sets technical/safety standards; EAC licenses charging providers and sets tariffs; EDC supplies power. [source-claim]
- EAC price ceiling (effective Oct 2025): AC 1,050 KHR/kWh (~$0.26); DC up to 250 kW 1,350; DC over 250 kW 1,450. Down from 1,600 in Aug 2023. Discounts allowed, premiums not. [verified] (Kiripost, Freelibrary, others)
- Licensed operators reportedly buy from EDC below consumer tariff, with no transformer, deposit or connection fee. Not confirmed; EDC wholesale rate not found. [source-claim]
- Tax: BEV import duty fully exempt from 1 April 2026; batteries and charging equipment tariffs removed. [verified] (several outlets)

## Competitors / players
- EDC: planned 30 more stations by Jan 2026, at least one per province. Also a possible partner. [source-claim]
- 18 flash-charging stations being built in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Sihanoukville by end 2026. [source-claim]
- EVCambodia and brand-owned chargers: not yet researched.

## What this means for CamGarage
- Price is capped, so charger margin = electricity cost x utilization. Our own chargers are a thin, capex-heavy business at this stage. [assumption]
- A platform (map + availability + parking) avoids licensing at the start. Whether showing prices or taking payments needs an EAC licence is unknown. [open]
- The cap makes price comparison useless as a feature; availability, connector type and parking are what differentiate.
- First segment: car EV owners and ride-hailing/fleet drivers in Phnom Penh. [assumption]
